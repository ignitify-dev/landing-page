Privacy Policy

What personal data Ignitify holds, why, and for how long. These are the sections the policy will contain — and one that is already answerable.

Already true

The personal-data surface is small.

This part is an engineering fact rather than a legal determination, so it can be stated now.

One field

A tenant record carries an owner email address. That is the platform’s personal-data surface — not a summary of it.

Redacted once

Redaction is applied at a single facade that every log and telemetry sink passes through, rather than per sink where one could be missed.

No tracking

This site sets one thing in your browser: a light/dark theme preference. No analytics, no advertising pixel, no third-party tag on any page.

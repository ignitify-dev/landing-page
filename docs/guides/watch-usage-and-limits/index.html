Watch usage and limits

See what your plan allows with whoami and quota. See what you used with usage, and take the data out with usage:export.

1 · See who you are and what the plan allows

whoami prints the tenant, then the limits of its plan. The number after the plan name is its version: it starts at 1 and goes up each time the terms of the plan change.

2 · See what you hold now

quota shows each live count against its limit, as used / max.

Note

quota counts what you hold right now, and usage is the ledger for a month.

3 · See what you used this month

usage prints one row per axis for the current month.

4 · Narrow it down

Pick a month with --period and an axis with --axis, filter by --vm or --run, and add --vms or --runs to list the lines behind the totals.

Warning

--run takes the run's UUID id, not the run_… id.

5 · Export the lines

usage:export prints one row per ledger line, in the one format you name: --csv or --json.

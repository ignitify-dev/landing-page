Keep a VM warm or pinned

A warm VM serves your next run without a cold boot, and a pinned VM stays until you delete it. Look at your VMs with vm:list and remove one with vm:delete.

1 · Declare it in task.yaml

Declare cardinality: many to reuse a VM between runs, and add residency: pinned to keep it until you delete it.

Warning

Pinning needs a plan that allows it: free/starter/team may not; pro/enterprise (on request) may. The samples with a pinned VM on this page, and on the vm pages of the CLI reference, come from a tenant on a plan that allows it.

2 · Run it and list your VMs

vm:list shows each live VM with its residency, state, age and idle time.

3 · Look at one VM

vm:get adds the boot time to the detail of one VM.

4 · Show only pinned VMs

--pinned lists the pins, and a ⚠ after the idle time marks a pin idle past the warning window, which is only a warning.

Note

A host restart ends a pin: vm:list --pinned reports it as lost, and the next run sets the pin again.

5 · Delete a VM

A pinned VM needs --force to unlock the delete, and -y skips the question that --force does not.

Presets & limits

A preset is a fixed VM size, chosen with run.preset in task.yaml. Your tier sets which presets you may use, and every other ceiling.

Running VMs is how many runs can be active at the same time. Units limits the total weight of those runs, and today only these plans have it: enterprise (on request). A plan with no units limit is still limited by its running VMs and its presets.

Two capabilities are gated per tier: pinning (residency: pinned: free/starter/team may not; pro/enterprise (on request) may) and inbound networking (network: bidirectional: free may not; starter/team/pro/enterprise (on request) may). A tier that has not unlocked one refuses it. See Refused values.

A higher ceiling is refused

A ceiling you declare above your tier's is refused, not clamped. This covers execution.storage_mib, inbound_rps and egress_mbit. Rate ceilings (deployments per minute, runs per day, API requests per second) are enforced live, not at deploy. See quota and usage.

Payload and result size

Two limits are the same for every plan. Both sizes are the compact JSON of the value. Ask ignitify whoami --json for the numbers (limits.max_payload_bytes and limits.max_result_bytes).

  • At most 10 KiB (10,240 bytes) goes into a task: the payload of a run, a webhook body, or a schedule.
  • At most 10 KiB (10,240 bytes) comes out: the result, an error or a progress chunk.

Over the limit, a payload is refused with 413 and the run does not start. A result over the limit fails the run (result exceeds max size). A webhook body over 10 KiB gets 413 payload too large before the signature is checked. task:validate and task:deploy refuse a run.payload in task.yaml that is over the limit.

Need to move more data?

Send it to your own store, for example S3, from inside the task and return a link. A task with network: none can only return 10 KiB.

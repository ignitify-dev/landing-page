What happens next

  1. Sign in

    Choose your Google account. We never see your password.

  2. Your free workspace is created

    The account page shows your plan, its limits and your keys. It never shows a key.

  3. Run ignitify login

    The CLI opens a page. Check the code, then choose Approve. Your key goes to your machine only through the CLI.
